# Key Insights

## Findings

- Analysed **400** texts: **56.8%** positive, **9.0%** neutral, **34.2%** negative (average score +0.26).
- Text sentiment matches the star rating **93%** of the time.
- Negative text is dominated by: **awful, customer, drains, support, delay**.
- Positive text is dominated by: **product, comfortable, sound, quick, fit**.
- Positive share moved **-7.6 percentage points** between the first and second half of the period.

## Recommendations

- Investigate the issues behind negative terms (awful, customer, drains) and track them in product and support reviews.
- Use positive themes (product, comfortable, sound) in marketing messages and product pages.
- Respond quickly to negative texts to recover unhappy customers.
- Re-run this analysis monthly to catch sentiment shifts early.
