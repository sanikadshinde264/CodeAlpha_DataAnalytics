# Key Insights

## Findings

- Analysed **20,000** texts: **88.3%** positive, **2.1%** neutral, **9.6%** negative (average score +0.66).
- Text sentiment matches the star rating **80%** of the time.
- Negative text is dominated by: **bad, disappointed, awful, problem, money**.
- Positive text is dominated by: **great, good, love, best, better**.
- Strongest emotions: **trust, joy, anticipation**.
- Positive share moved **+0.6 percentage points** between the first and second half of the period.

## Recommendations

- Investigate the issues behind negative terms (bad, disappointed, awful) and track them in product and support reviews.
- Use positive themes (great, good, love) in marketing messages and product pages.
- Respond quickly to negative texts to recover unhappy customers.
- Re-run this analysis monthly to catch sentiment shifts early.
